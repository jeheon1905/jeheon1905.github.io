§ 01

About

I develop algorithms and software at the intersection of quantum chemistry, high-performance computing, and machine learning—building tools that make first-principles simulation faster, more accurate, and more useful for chemistry.

I am an AI Fellow (AI Assistant Professor) at the Center for Artificial Intelligence and Natural Sciences at the Korea Institute for Advanced Study (KIAS). Before joining KIAS in May 2026, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Division of National Supercomputing at the Korea Institute of Science and Technology Information (KISTI).

I received my Ph.D. in Chemistry from KAIST in February 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Woo Youn Kim, with a dissertation on accelerating quantum chemistry calculations through algorithmic optimization on advanced hardware. I hold dual B.S. degrees in Chemistry and Physics from Hanyang University.

My work appears in journal publications as well as top AI conference proceedings. For a complete academic record, see my CV.

Quantum Chemistry & DFT

Real-space methods, eigensolvers, preconditioning, basis-set design

High-Performance Computing

GPU acceleration, multi-GPU eigenvalue solvers, mixed-precision algorithms

AI for Science

Neural pseudopotentials, generative models for transition states & molecular geometry

Scientific Software

Open-source quantum chemistry packages, scalable numerical methods
